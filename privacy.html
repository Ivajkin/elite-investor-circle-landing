Privacy

Elite Investor Circle is an initiative of Timothy Ivaikin, part of the Edge Ecosystem partner network.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the contact form: name, email, role, company and message.

Why

To reply to your note and, if there is a fit, to continue the conversation about working with Edge.

Where it goes

The form is delivered by Formspree (formspree.io) to our email. Relevant notes are copied into the Edge CRM. We do not sell or share your details with anyone without your consent.

How long

We keep notes for as long as needed to reply and follow up, and no longer than 3 years.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete your details, write to us through the contact form and say what you want changed.
