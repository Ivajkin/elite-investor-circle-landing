ABOUT

Fewer, better conversations

The idea behind Elite Investor Circle: fewer, better conversations between founders, executives and the people who back them, based on facts. Today it is part of the Edge partner network. Edge Ecosystem builds CRM, portals, integrations and automation for growing companies.

How it works

Three ways to take part

For companies

Tell us where your company loses time or money. Edge proposes a CRM, portal, integration or automation project with a clear scope and price.

For partners

If you know a company that needs such a system, introduce it with its consent. Partnerships run on written agreements.

For founders and executives

Small private roundtables in Moscow and online, where people share real cases. Leave a note to be invited.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about Elite Investor Circle.

Can I invest or earn money through Elite Investor Circle?

No. We do not accept money, manage assets, sell securities or give investment advice. If anyone asks you to invest money in the name of Elite Investor Circle, it is not us.

Who should write to us?

Founders and executives of companies that want to grow with better systems, and partners who know such companies.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There is no membership fee and no investment threshold.

What happens after I write?

We read every note and reply when there is a fit.
